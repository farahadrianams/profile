About

I am Farah Adriana Mohd Samsul, an MPhil student in Electrical Engineering at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia. I completed my Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical-Electronics at UTM, where I built a foundation in electronics, programming, and engineering design.

My research focuses on designing and optimizing a fiber optic daylighting system to improve indoor natural light distribution and reduce reliance on artificial lighting. I use Zemax ray-tracing simulations to evaluate concentrator performance, study illuminance distribution, and guide design improvements.
